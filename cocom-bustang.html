CoCoM Bustang Intercity Coach

Highway Transit Integration Concept for Colorado

CoCo (Colorado Connected) is an independent, speculative statewide transit advocacy master plan developed to demonstrate how passenger rail and regional buses could seamlessly integrate across Colorado. In this concept proposal, Bustang intercity coaches coordinate directly with passenger rail timetables and unified fares along I-25, I-70, and US-50.

Single-Leg Fare Only • Strict Transfer Add-On Required

Standard point-to-point tickets aboard CoCoM Bustang Coach cover travel on this leg only. Transfers to connecting passenger rail, Bustang coaches, or local municipal systems are NOT included. Connecting passengers must add the Universal Transfer Add-On (+$3.00 Local / +$5.00 Express) or hold an active Monthly C&A Pass.

MCI Mountain Spec

  • Engine: Cummins X12 11.9-liter turbo-diesel producing 455 hp and 1,700 lb-ft torque.
  • Transmission: Allison B500 Gen-V 6-speed automatic with hydraulic retarder.
  • Mountain Braking: Jacobs engine compression brake + Wabco disc brakes with ABS/ESC.
