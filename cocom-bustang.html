CoCoM Bustang Intercity Coach

Connecting Colorado's Critical Interstate Corridors

CoCoM Bustang operates state-of-the-art Motor Coach Industries (MCI) luxury intercity coaches across the West Line (I-70 Mountain Corridor), North Line (I-25 to Fort Collins), and South Line (I-25 to Colorado Springs and Pueblo). Equipped with plush reclining seats, 110V outlets, 5G Wi-Fi, onboard lavatory, cavernous underfloor luggage bays, and dual front-mounted bike racks.

Powertrain & Safety

MCI Mountain Spec

  • Engine: Cummins X12 11.9-liter turbo-diesel producing 455 hp and 1,700 lb-ft torque.
  • Transmission: Allison B500 Gen-V 6-speed automatic with hydraulic retarder.
  • Mountain Braking: Jacobs engine compression brake + Wabco disc brakes with ABS/ESC.
Rider Comfort

First-Class Highway Ride

  • Climate Control: High-capacity Carrier rooftop dual HVAC with multi-stage air purification.
  • Connectivity: High-gain 5G Wi-Fi with dual cellular carrier SIM aggregation.
  • Restroom: Full recirculating lavatory with antibacterial soap dispensers and infant changing table.
Cargo & Capacity

Luggage, Skis & Bikes

  • Underfloor Bays: 3 cavernous pass-through luggage compartments (up to 2 bags per passenger free).
  • Front Bike Rack: Dual heavy-duty bike carrier accommodates mountain bikes and road bikes.
  • Overhead Parcels: Deep enclosed overhead racks for daypacks, coats, and laptop bags.
