CoCoM Pegasus Mountain Micro-Transit

Fast, Agile High-Frequency Mountain Van Transit

CoCoM Pegasus is Colorado’s nimble mountain micro-transit solution designed to bypass weekend gridlock on I-70. Utilizing customized Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 3500 & Ford Transit AWD vans, Pegasus holds exclusive CDOT authorization to operate toll-free in the I-70 Mountain Express Lanes (Hard Shoulder Running), cutting up to an hour off peak ski commutes.

CDOT Express Privilege

Toll-Free Shoulder Access

  • Express Lanes: Authorized for toll-free usage of eastbound & westbound I-70 Mountain Express Lanes.
  • Hard Shoulder Running: Certified to legally utilize reinforced paved shoulders when general lanes halt.
