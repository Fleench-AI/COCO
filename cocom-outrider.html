Severe Terrain Engineering

  • Engine: Cummins L9 Clean Diesel generating 330 hp and 1,000 lb-ft torque.
  • Suspension: Ridewell heavy-duty air suspension with automatic ride height leveling over gravel roads.
  • Clearance: High-profile underbody protection shields fuel tanks and exhaust from icy mountain ruts.
Equity Accessibility

Direct Low-Floor Ramp

  • Zero Steps: Full low-floor entry eliminates steep coach stair climbing for seniors and riders with strollers.
  • Ramp Slope: 1:6 ultra-gentle grade ensures effortless manual and power wheelchair entry.
  • Automated Kneeling: Pneumatic front suspension kneels 4 inches to touch rural curb edges.
State Subsidized

Equity Fare Guarantee

  • Equity Fare Cap: Subsidized by Colorado SB-260 transit funding, capping rural lifeline fares at $11–$40.
  • Medical Access: Direct connections to VA clinics, regional cancer centers, and dialysis facilities.
  • C&A Card Offline: Cards cache trips even in zero-cell coverage valleys along US-50 and US-285.
