Complete Statewide Card Matrix (C, Y, A, U)

Designed for 100% statewide coverage across all 64 Colorado counties. Every tier is offered in either free standard durable recycled plastic or a premium battery-free Plus (+) e-ink companion.

The Default Adult Transit Pass (Ages 18+)
100% Fare

The backbone transit credential for Colorado adults. Standard C cards are issued completely free at every automated station kiosk and hub desk, operating 100% anonymously when unlinked or with zero-liability loss protection in the CoCo app.

Standard C Card (Free) Offline flash storage holds 5 tickets/passes + cash balance. Ready for instant offline validation.

Engineering • Hardware Specifications

Technical Specifications & Architectural Breakdown

Engineering breakdown of how Colorado's universal fare media functions without chemical batteries, maintains visibility in blinding high-altitude sunshine, and cryptographically proves ticket validity deep inside mountain rail tunnels.

ISO/IEC 14443 & 15693 Magnetic Induction

100% Battery-Free NFC Energy Harvesting

Plus (+) smartcards contain zero chemical batteries, eliminating toxic disposal, fire risks, and cold-weather battery death. An integrated planar copper PCB antenna coil captures energy from the 13.56 MHz RF field of any transit validator or smartphone during the 300-millisecond tap.

  • ✓ 300ms Energy Burst: Inductive current powers the secure element co-processor and completes the microcapsule display refresh in a single tap.
  • ✓ Rapid Solid-State Capacitor: Delivers 3.3V @ 12mA without chemical degradation, surviving over 1,000,000 refresh cycles.
  • ✓ 10+ Year Hardware Lifespan: Without a battery to expire or leak, cards remain operational for over a decade.
Electrophoretic Microcapsule Technology

Bi-Stable E-Ink Display (-25°F Cold Rated)

Utilizes electrophoretic microcapsules where charged titanium dioxide (white) and carbon (black) particles physically migrate under electrical charge. Once particles move, they stay anchored indefinitely with zero standby power.

  • ✓ 0 Watts Standby Draw: Display retains balance, tap verification, and Snack Zone barcode indefinitely even when stored in a drawer.
  • ✓ 14,000-ft Glare-Free Clarity: 100% reflective ambient light visibility under Colorado's intense alpine sunshine.
  • ✓ -25°F Cold Formulation: Specialized low-viscosity electrophoretic fluid certified for sub-zero Colorado mountain winters.
Sub-300ms Offline Flash Commit

Offline Tap-and-Sync Architecture

Over 40% of Colorado's mountain passenger routes cross zero-cellular coverage dead zones (Moffat Tunnel, Glenwood Canyon, Wolf Creek Pass). The system eliminates cellular dependencies by storing active tickets and cash balances locally in secure flash storage.

  • ✓ Instant Onboard Tariff Engine: Readers inspect local pass validity, calculate demographic discounts, and commit write cycles in under 300ms.
  • ✓ Asynchronous Ledger Sync: Encrypted transaction logs cache locally on vehicles and batch-upload upon reaching cellular or depot Wi-Fi.
  • ✓ 5 Ticket Local Cache: Standard C and Plus C+ cards hold up to 5 pre-purchased tickets directly in hardware memory.
ECDSA Digital Proof-of-Payment

Platform Validators & Handheld Scanners

For open-boarding systems like RTD Light Rail platforms and high-volume CoCoR rail stops, riders tap yellow totem platform validators before boarding. The validator writes an authenticated digital timestamp directly into the card's local storage sector.

  • ✓ Offline Fare Inspection: Fare inspectors carry ruggedized offline handheld scanners that inspect and cryptographically verify timestamps.
  • ✓ Anti-Cloning Monotonic Counters: Every tap increments a tamper-proof hardware counter protected by an EAL6+ secure element.
  • ✓ Single-Use Paper RFID/QR: Infrequent visitors and unbanked riders purchase single-use paper tickets with identical tap validation.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Operating guidelines for Colorado's proposed 4-tier transit media and electronic ink platform.

How does the 18th Birthday Swap work?

To eliminate digital fraud and verify adulthood, Youth (Y) cards do not automatically graduate in software on your 18th birthday. At age 18, bring your physical Y card to any station kiosk or staffed Main Desk to graduate to an adult Core (C) card. Plus Y+ cardholders receive an exclusive 50% discount voucher toward an adult Plus C+ e-ink upgrade!

How do University cards work without new plastic?

CoCo transit credentials provision directly onto a designated, secure, unused NFC sector of your existing campus ID card (CU Buff OneCard, CSU RamCard, CMU MAVcard, etc.). Real-time registrar sync verifies in-state student enrollment, automatically applying the 30% student discount.

Do I ever need to charge a Plus (+) card battery?

Never. Plus (+) cards contain 100% battery-free bistable electrophoretic e-ink displays. The display requires zero power to retain its image, and momentarily harvests ambient RF magnetic induction energy during your 300ms tap at validators, kiosks, or smartphones to update your balance and screen.

What is the State Guaranteed Floor for local agencies?

Local transit partners (like RTD) retain full right to set local fares, such as RTD Zero Fare for Youth. However, the state establishes a legal 50% discount floor for Youth and Seniors across every partnered local system. If a local system charges standard fares, Y Card taps for half price, and CoCo automatically reimburses the difference to make the local agency whole.
