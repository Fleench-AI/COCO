Proposed Hardware Architecture • Technical Specifications

Technical Specifications & Architectural Breakdown

Detailed engineering breakdown of how Colorado's universal fare media functions without batteries, maintains visibility in blinding alpine sunlight, and cryptographically proves ticket validity deep inside mountain rail tunnels.

⚡
ISO/IEC 14443 & 15693 Magnetic Induction

100% Battery-Free NFC Energy Harvesting

The C & A card contains zero chemical batteries, eliminating toxic lithium disposal, fire hazards, and winter battery death. When the card passes within 4 cm of an CoCo platform validator or NFC-enabled smartphone, an integrated planar PCB coil harvests energy directly from the 13.56 MHz RF electromagnetic field.

  • ✓ Rapid Charge Capacitor: Harvests 3.3V @ 12mA in under 45 milliseconds to refresh the screen.
  • ✓ Dual-Standard Support: Complies with both ISO 14443 Type A (transit gates) and ISO 15693 (extended range inventory).
  • ✓ Indefinite Lifespan: Without a battery to degrade, physical cards are certified for 10+ years of daily use.
📄
E Ink Carta™ Electrophoretic Microcapsules

Bi-Stable Electronic Ink Microcapsules

Unlike power-draining OLED or LCD smartcards, the C & A card utilizes micro-encapsulated electrophoretic display (EPD) technology. Positively charged white titanium dioxide particles and negatively charged black carbon particles move into place under momentary electrical excitation and stay physically anchored forever without power.

  • ✓ Zero Standby Draw: Display maintains your balance and seat assignment even if left in a drawer for years.
  • ✓ Glir-Free Alpine Sunlight: Reflective paper display offers 100% legibility in blinding 14,000-ft Colorado sunshine.
  • ✓ Electrophoretic Wash: Firmware cleans microcapsule ghosting with an automatic 3-phase inversion cycle.
🔐
ECDSA-P256 Asymmetric Encryption

Offline Mountain Tunnel Cryptographic Security

Over 40% of Colorado's mountain passenger routes cross areas with zero cellular reception—such as the 6.2-mile Moffat Tunnel, Glenwood Canyon, and Wolf Creek Pass. The C & A card solves this by rendering an encrypted Aztec 2D matrix barcode directly on the E-ink display upon boarding.

  • ✓ Conductor Offline Verification: Conductor optical scanners verify the card's ECDSA signature without internet.
  • ✓ Anti-Cloning Counter: Each ticket generation increments an encrypted internal monotonic hardware counter.
  • ✓ Tamper-Resistant Secure Element: EAL6+ certified cryptographic co-processor protects user private keys.
❄️
IP68 Submersible • -25°F Operational Rating

Sub-Zero Alpine Climate & Physical Durability

Engineered specifically for Colorado winter conditions. The card is laminated in a multi-tier polycarbonate and TPU polymer matrix that resists freeze-thaw cycles, high-altitude UV radiation, and heavy road de-icing salts (magnesium chloride).

  • ✓ -25°F Temperature Rating: Microcapsule electrophoretic fluid formulated to prevent freezing down to -31.6°C.
  • ✓ Bend & Torsion Resistant: Flexible display substrate survives 10,000+ pocket flexes without fracturing.
  • ✓ IP68 Waterproof: 100% submersible in snowmelt and puddle slush; no battery compartment seals to fail.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about Colorado's electronic ink transit media.

Do I ever need to plug the card into a charger?

No. The card has zero batteries and no charging ports. The electronic ink screen draws zero watts while holding an image. It receives instantaneous micro-bursts of power purely through NFC radio waves whenever tapped against a transit validator or scanned by your smartphone.

How does the 2-hour transfer rule work?

When you tap onto any CoCoR passenger train or CoCoM bus, the system writes a cryptographic timestamp to your card starting a 2-hour transfer window. Transferring to any connecting local line or partner agency (RTD, Mountain Metro, Bustang) within that window is completely free ($0.00).

What if I lose my physical C & A card?

If your card is registered to your account, your balance is 100% protected under standard transit balance protection policies. Simply log into the portal to freeze the lost card instantly. A replacement card will be issued with your full balance restored immediately.

Can I use my employer pre-tax transit dollars (IRC § 132f)?

Yes. You can link any IRS Code § 132(f) Qualified Transportation Fringe commuter benefit debit card (WageWorks, Edenred, HealthEquity) directly to auto-reload your C & A card balance, saving up to 35% on Colorado transit through pre-tax payroll deductions.
