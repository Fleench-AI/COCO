Strict Transfer Add-On Policy • CoCoM Snowstang Ski: Single-Leg Point-to-Point Fare Structure — Click to review transfer rules & add-on requirements
▾

Base tickets aboard this service are single-leg point-to-point only and DO NOT include transfer privileges. To connect between passenger rail and regional bus lines (or between corridors), passengers must purchase the Universal Transfer Add-On (+$3.00 Local / +$5.00 Express) or hold an active Monthly C&A Pass.

Transfer add-ons allow a 2-hour multi-agency connection window across CDOT Bustang, RTD Metro, and connecting regional shuttles statewide.

Extreme Winter Spec

Automated Insta-Chains

  • Traction Chains: Insta-Chain pneumatic rotational chain system deploys in 3 seconds at highway speed.
  • Anti-Icing: Thermally heated DEF tanks, fuel line heaters, and heated air dryer reservoirs prevent mountain freeze-ups.
  • Mountain Braking: Jacobs engine brake and hydraulic transmission retarder ensure safe descents on 7% grades.
Gear Protection

Heated Luggage Caverns

  • Capacity: Holds up to 100 pairs of skis or snowboards in custom-molded protective racks.
  • Climate Controlled: Hydronic heaters continuously circulate warmth to thaw gear before arrival.
  • Equipment Included: Skis, poles, snowboards, and boot bags travel 100% free with round-trip ticket.
Curbside Privilege

Direct Resort Base Drop

  • Base Access: Pulls directly up to the base lodge at Loveland, A-Basin, Copper, Breckenridge, & Steamboat.
  • No Parking Hassle: Avoid $30–$50 resort parking fees and overcrowded outer lot shuttle buses.
  • Guaranteed Return: Coach holds your reserved seat for the return trip to Denver departing after the lifts close.
