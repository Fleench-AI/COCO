CoCoRX Express Passenger Rail (COR-X)

Proposed High-Speed Corridor Transit Uniting Colorado’s Anchor Metros

CoCo (Colorado Connected) is an independent, speculative statewide transit advocacy master plan developed to demonstrate how passenger rail and regional buses could seamlessly integrate across Colorado. CoCoRX Express envisions rapid intercity rail between Northern Colorado, Denver, Colorado Springs, and Pueblo with 125 mph express trainsets, quiet work lounges, and guaranteed electronic seat reservations.

Traction & Performance

125 mph High-Speed Power

  • Power System: Dual-mode 25kV 60Hz AC electric catenary + 4,400 hp Cummins QSK95 Tier 4 diesel generator.
  • Acceleration: 0 to 60 mph in 42 seconds for rapid departure from hub terminals.
  • Grade Performance: Maintained 90 mph over the 3.5% grades of Palmer Divide & Raton Foothills.
Guaranteed Seating

Electronic E-Ink Seat Tags

  • Seat Pitch: Spacious 38-inch legroom pitch (First Class: 42-inch pitch).
  • Live E-Ink Screens: Seats display rider names and booked segment automatically upon ticket purchase.

CoCoRX Express Pricing & Value Guarantee

To guarantee passenger seating comfort and high-speed bypass priority, CoCoRX Express fares apply a +25% premium above the CoCoR Local base. Every express ticket includes an electronic reserved seat, complimentary Starlink satellite Wi-Fi, and access to the Whisper Zone.
