Service Overview & Philosophy

CoCoR Local (COR) trains serve every station community along Colorado's passenger rail corridors without exception. Engineered with high-capacity bi-level and single-level modern rail cars, COR connects towns like Loveland, Longmont, Castle Rock, Fountain, and Walsenburg into the state economy with reliable, weather-resilient hourly service.

Pricing is intentionally set 10% lower than comparable Amtrak long-distance coach baselines to guarantee passenger rail is an affordable everyday choice for Colorado families and daily commuters.

Included Amenities & Policies

🚲 Free CoCo Cargo Dedicated roll-on bike racks and ski storage in every rail car at zero extra charge.
🪑 Unreserved Seating Walk-on flexibility with your C & A Card. No blackout dates, surge pricing, or seat lock-ins.
🔌 Universal Power Standard 110V AC and dual USB-C rapid charging ports at all 2x2 passenger seats.
📶 5G Mesh Wi-Fi Continuous high-speed statewide data connection for seamless remote work and streaming.

The Express Experience

CoCoRX Express (COR-X) is designed for intercity business travellers and travelers prioritizing time. Operating on dedicated bypass tracks along the Front Range spine and major regional corridors, COR-X skips minor waypoints to shave up to 35% off trip times.

Every COR-X ticket comes with a guaranteed assigned seat, eliminating boarding rushes. Ticket holders can choose standard coach or book directly into the dedicated Quiet Work Lounge car.

Premium Cabin Amenities

🤫 Quiet Work Lounge Cell phone calls strictly prohibited. Ergonomic high-back leather seats and whisper-quiet HVAC.

High-Frequency Mountain Shuttles

Pegasus micro-transit vans operate along the I-70 Mountain Corridor departing from Denver Union Station and the Denver Federal Center. Built on custom 14-passenger Mercedes-Benz Sprinter chassis, Pegasus vehicles are legally authorized to travel in the I-70 Mountain Express Lanes for free, blowing past weekend ski gridlock.

Departures run every 60 minutes on peak Thursday through Monday schedules, providing a nimble car-free gateway into Summit and Eagle counties.

CDOT Civil Rights • C.R.S. Title 43 Article 4

Accessibility, Passenger Rights & Rider Policies

Colorado is dedicated to providing barrier-free, equitable, and dignified public transit for all residents and visitors across every county and mountain corridor.

♿

ADA Universal Accessibility

All CoCoR passenger rail cars feature 100% level boarding at Tier 1 and Tier 2 stations, dedicated tie-down spaces with universal power docks, and ADA-compliant accessible restrooms. Bustang and Pegasus motorcoaches are equipped with automated BraunAbility wheelchair lifts.

Zero reservation required for ADA spaces.
🐕

Service Animals Policy

Trained service animals accompanying passengers with disabilities ride free on all CoCo vehicles without restriction. Small pets inside fully enclosed, leak-proof airline-compliant pet carriers that fit beneath seats are permitted at no extra charge.

Compliant with 49 C.F.R. Part 37.
⛷️

CoCo Cargo: Ski & Bike Gear

In accordance with Colorado outdoor recreation priorities, passengers may bring up to two pairs of skis/snowboards or one bicycle completely free of charge. CoCoR trains feature dedicated end-car racks; Bustang coaches provide underfloor climate-controlled luggage bays.

Free gear transit on all corridors.
🎒

Zero Fare for Colorado Youth

Pursuant to state ozone mitigation and youth equity statutes, all individuals aged 18 and under ride 100% free statewide on all standard CoCoR trains and CoCoM buses. Youth passes can be loaded directly onto physical C & A Smart Cards or paired with Colorado student IDs.

Mandated under C.R.S. § 43-4-1102.
⚖️

Title VI Non-Discrimination

The Colorado Department of Transportation (CDOT) operates its transit programs without regard to race, color, national origin, sex, age, or disability in accordance with Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964. Free translation and interpretation services are available in Spanish, Vietnamese, and 12 other languages.

CDOT Civil Rights Office: (303) 757-9234.
🛡️

Passenger Code of Conduct

To maintain a comfortable transit environment, smoking/vaping, alcohol consumption outside designated dining cars, and disruptive audio devices without headphones are strictly prohibited. Fare inspection is conducted randomly by authorized state transit fare marshals.
