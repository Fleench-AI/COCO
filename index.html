CoCoR Local Rail (COR)

The passenger rail backbone serving all local communities along the Front Range (FR), Northeast (NE), Eastern Plains (EP), Southern (SL), and Arkansas Valley (AV) lines.

  • ✓ Free CoCo Cargo bike & ski racks in every car
  • ✓ Walk-up unreserved open seating with no surge pricing
  • ✓ 10% cheaper than standard Amtrak coach baseline

CoCoRX Express Rail (COR-X)

High-speed express passenger rail connecting primary anchor hubs (Fort Collins, Denver, Colo Springs, Pueblo, Grand Junction) bypassing smaller stations.

  • ✓ Guaranteed seat reservation included with every ticket
  • ✓ Dedicated Quiet Work Lounge car (whisper-zone)
  • ✓ Starlink satellite Wi-Fi & CoCo Snacks high-top bar

CoCoM Bustang

CDOT's established express motorcoach backbone connecting Denver across West Line (I-70 to Grand Jct), North Line (Fort Collins), and South Line (Colo Springs & Pueblo).

  • ✓ 50-passenger MCI motorcoaches with onboard restroom
  • ✓ 110V power outlets at every seat & free 5G Wi-Fi
  • ✓ Undercarriage luggage bays & 3-bike front racks
Explore Bustang
Mountain Micro-Transit $13.00 – $20.00

CoCoM Pegasus

High-frequency 14-passenger custom Sprinter passenger vans authorized to use the I-70 Mountain Express Lanes to bypass peak weekend ski traffic between Denver, Frisco, Vail, and Avon.

  • ✓ Toll-free authorization in I-70 Mountain Express Lanes
  • ✓ Hourly weekend departures from Denver Union Station
  • ✓ Exterior ski/snowboard racks and USB charging ports
Explore Pegasus
Rural Lifeline Transit $11.00 – $40.00

CoCoM Outrider

Subsidized rural transit connecting remote mountain communities and agricultural plains—Craig, Crested Butte, Alamosa, Durango, Lamar, and Sterling—ensuring statewide mobility for all Coloradans.

  • ✓ 10 comprehensive long-distance statewide corridors
  • ✓ Mountain-capable high-clearance motorcoaches
  • ✓ Heavily subsidized fares ensuring healthcare access
Explore Outrider
Seasonal Ski Express $25 – $40 Round-Trip

CoCoM Snowstang

Direct weekend winter ski buses from Denver Union Station and Denver Federal Center to chairlift bases at Loveland, Arapahoe Basin, Copper Mountain, Breckenridge, and Steamboat.

  • ✓ Direct drop-off at resort chairlift bases (no resort parking fee)
  • ✓ Guaranteed round-trip reserved seat and heated gear storage
  • ✓ Operates every Saturday, Sunday & Holiday Monday (Dec–May)
