Pursuant to Colorado Revised Statutes (C.R.S. Title 43), the state coordinates and operates comprehensive passenger rail and intercity mobility services. Uniting high-speed Front Range express rail, local passenger lines, Bustang motorcoaches, Pegasus mountain shuttles, and rural transit corridors into one seamless public service.
