Colorado Connected (CoCo) • Vision Transit Master Plan

CoCo (Colorado Connected) is an independent, speculative statewide transit advocacy master plan developed to demonstrate how passenger rail and regional buses could seamlessly integrate across Colorado. Uniting high-speed Front Range express rail, local passenger lines, Bustang motorcoaches, Pegasus mountain shuttles, and rural transit corridors into one synchronized public service vision.
