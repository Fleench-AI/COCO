STUDY NOTICE #2026-CO-04 Published November 2026 • Independent Civic Review

Draft Statewide Rail Alignment & Clean Dual-Mode Fleet Environmental Review

The CoCo Independent Transit Planning Study has published its comprehensive Phase III corridor alignment review, evaluating passenger rail operations along existing BNSF and Union Pacific trackage shared corridors. The report details noise abatement through quiet zones in Loveland, Longmont, and Colorado Springs, zero-emission battery-electric multiple units (BEMU) within urban terminal air basins, and grade separation priority at 14 high-volume Front Range arterial crossings.

Public Comment Period: Open through January 15, 2027

Members of the public, municipal transportation planners, and regional mobility advocates are invited to review the draft timetables and submit formal written commentary on station dwell times, early-morning airport commuter connections, and ADA low-floor platform specifications.

STATUTORY CONTEXT

Alignment with Colorado Senate Bill 21-260 & Front Range Passenger Rail District

This independent proposal is architected to complement the statutory framework established under Colorado SB 21-260 (Sustainability of the Transportation System) and SB 24-184 (Support for Passenger Rail Service).

Key Legal Boundaries:
  • Non-governmental, independent study vision.
  • Does not supersede CDOT or FRPRD official board filings.
  • All fares model state equity mandates (50% Youth/Senior, 30% College discounts).
  • Official public hearings are held exclusively by the State at codot.gov.
