CoCoR Local Passenger Rail (COR)

The Proposed Community Rail Backbone of Colorado

CoCo (Colorado Connected) is an independent, speculative statewide transit advocacy master plan developed to demonstrate how passenger rail and regional buses could seamlessly integrate across Colorado. CoCoR Local Passenger Rail envisions frequent, all-stops passenger heavy rail transit connecting Front Range downtowns, agricultural river valleys, and mountain gateways with dual-mode trainsets and level-boarding accessibility.

Fleet & Equipment Specifications

Stadler FLIRT / KISS Dual-Mode Multiple Unit (DMU/BMU)

Standard 4-Car Articulated Trainset Consist Layout

Jakobs Bogie Articulated Design

Every CoCoR Local train is composed of an articulated 4-car consist with ultra-wide continuous gangways and shared Jakobs bogies for silent, vibration-free travel. Low-floor boarding doors are aligned directly with station platforms across Colorado.

Trainset Model
Stadler FLIRT / KISS

High-efficiency articulated Dual-Mode Multiple Unit (DMU/BMU). Built by Stadler Rail in Salt Lake City, optimized for North American FRA alternate compliance Tier I standards.

⚡
Maximum Speed
110 mph (177 km/h)

Operates on upgraded Class 5 and Class 6 welded jointless rail with Positive Train Control (PTC) and automated speed enforcement, cutting Front Range highway travel times.

🌱
Dual-Mode Propulsion
Overhead 25kV AC + Tier 4

Dual-mode propulsion equipped with pantographs for 25kV AC overhead catenary electrification, plus low-emission EPA Tier 4 clean diesel-electric power modules for non-electrified track.

💺
Seating & Comfort
2x2 Ergonomic Contour

34-inch seat pitch (substantially more legroom than commuter rail), high-back lumbar support, fold-down tray tables, unreserved open walk-up seating, and large panoramic anti-glare windows.

♿
Accessibility & Cargo
100% Low-Floor Boarding

Zero-step boarding via wide double-leaf plug doors. Dedicated CoCo Cargo car accommodates 16 bicycles or 24 pairs of skis/snowboards. ADA universal restroom with hands-free automated door.

📶
Onboard Technology
5G Wi-Fi & 110V AC Outlets

High-speed complimentary 5G passenger Wi-Fi network, 110V AC power outlets and USB-A & USB-C fast charging at every seat pair, plus real-time dynamic ceiling-mounted passenger info screens.
